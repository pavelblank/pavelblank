### 🙋 About me

I build **self-hosted, local-first tools**. No subscriptions, no cloud lock in. Just software that runs on your own computer, keeps your data with you and respects your privacy.

I am not a traditional software developer. I design each tool around a real need, run it on my own machine every day, and build it with coding assistants.

Everything here is free to use and change. The only ask is that the author credit stays.

### ⭐ Latest: AutoPost Automation 3.0

**Social media automation that runs on your own computer.** A team of 14 agents researches topics, writes the posts, makes the pictures and videos, checks every fact, publishes to your accounts on a daily schedule and then checks that each post is really live. When the computer is idle it makes posts ahead of time, and it cleans up after itself.

- One click installer: `INSTALL.bat` checks your computer, installs what is missing and tells you what is ready
- Connects to Telegram, Bluesky, Discord, Mastodon, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Threads, LinkedIn, WordPress, X and, through a webhook, almost anything else
- A live dashboard with an agent office, an organisation chart and a system map
- Safe by default: it starts paused and publishes nothing until you say so
